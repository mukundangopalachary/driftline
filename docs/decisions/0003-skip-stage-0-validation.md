# 0003: Skip Stage 0 validation

- Date: 2026-11-18
- Decided by: owner
- Status: accepted

## Decision

Skip the validation work in Stage 0: customer interviews and the manual pilot. Proceed to building.

## What is skipped

- Contact list and 5+ interviews
- Manual 2-week pilot
- Exit criteria "3 people describe a real incident" and "1 pilot alert acted on"

## Known risk accepted

`plan.md` lists "skipping validation because building feels more productive" as the Stage 0 risk. `product.md` risks 4 (cold start) and 6 (willingness to pay) stay unvalidated. `competitors.md` shows the changelog-watching half is already crowded, so the differentiator (impact on the customer's own usage) is untested with real users.

## Kept

- `docs/validation/` kit stays in the repo, unused, in case the owner revisits.
- `docs/competitors.md` stays. Hands-on trials of competitors are not done.

## Revisit

Owner can reopen at any time, for example before Stage 3 (first usable product) or before charging anyone.
